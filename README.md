<h1>ROLL BASEBALL</h1>
<h2>Roll Baseball is web-based baseball game that uses dice rolling to control the batting and pitching.</h2>

<h2>Core Mechanics</h2>
<h3>Rolling</h3>
<p>Batting, pitching, stealing and tagging are all controlled by pressing the roll button. When pressed, it rolls the two dice displayed above the button. Events such as a HomeRun or strike out are based on the results rolled (see Rules below for further explanation)</p>

<h2>Rules</h2>
<h3>General</h3>
<p>A team scores a point when a batter advances 4 bases.</p>
<p>If a player gets a STRIKEOUT, then they  are removed from the field</p>
<p>After 3 STRIKEOUTS, the teams switch sides. There are 9 innings total, whoever has the highest points, wins.</p>

<h3>Batting</h3>
<p>Rolling two matching colors on the die instantly advances the batter by the following amount<br>ORANGE: HOME RUN (4 bases)<br>BLUE: TRIPLE (3 bases)<br>GREEN: DOUBLE (2 bases)<br>PURPLE: NONE (no bonus)</p>
<p>Otherwise, the roll total must be greater than or equal to STRIKE to become a SINGLE. Else it will result in a STRIKEOUT</p>

<h3>Pitching</h3>
<p>When pitching, any matching colors results in a STRIKEOUT for the enemy team.</p><p>Otherwise, the roll total must be greater than or equal to STRIKE to become a SINGLE, Else it will result in a DOUBLE for the enemy team.</p>

<h3>Special (Stealing/Tag Out</h3>
<p>STEAL and TAG OUT are both rolled with two normal/6 sided die. The roll total must be greater than or equal to SPECIAL to be a successful STEAL/TAG OUT respectively.</p>
<p>A successful STEAL advances the batter by 1 base, failure results in an OUT for that player</p>
<p>A successful TAG OUT instantly OUTs that player, failure results in that player advancing 1 base</p>

<h3>Die Breakdown</h3>
<p>There are three die types, POWER (Triangle, 4 sides), NORMAL (Square, 6 sides), CONTACT (Diamond, 8 sides).</p>
<p>Each side is assigned a number and a color, either ORANGE, BLUE, GREEN or PURPLE (See pitching/batting for bonuses)</p>

<h2>Installation Instructions</h2>
<p>None if playing on web, simple hit the link and play. If downloading the entire Godot Project, run the main_menu scene.</p>
